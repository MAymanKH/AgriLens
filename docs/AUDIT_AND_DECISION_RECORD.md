## Candidate-site evidence status

The original matrix's cross-site CMR counts, heat totals, WaPOR pilot assertions and rice-regulation rankings are not retained here as verified comparative findings: the repository lacks a complete reproducible evidence bundle for every site and every quality-filtered acquisition. This removes the unsupported ranking certainty without silently selecting a new site. The Lead can retain the existing pilot for implementation while Research supplies a defensible comparison.

The submitted representative points were Zankalon (30.58057, 31.43332), Side Ghazi (31.203, 30.988), Sakha (31.09853, 30.92250), and Mallawi (27.731, 30.842). These are comparison reference points, not verified demo farm polygons. A representative station coordinate is insufficient to infer field eligibility or crop-specific soil properties.

## Claims that may be handed to App/Presentation now

- AgriLens compares candidate crop sequences using declared indicative assumptions and historical gridded climate inputs.
- Local crop-water literature is relevant, but it must be separated from generic ranges and modeled ET0/ETc.
- Family diversity and legume presence describe the sequence; no quantified soil outcome follows from these flags.
- Acquisition metadata can guide later imagery review; it does not establish a cloud-free crop observation by itself.
- Rice permission and disputed agronomic parameters remain unverified and cannot support live recommendations.
